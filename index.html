iSpy World

A mobile learning application for young children that uses real-time object scanning (YOLOv11n) and interactive speech coaching to build foundational literacy and spelling.

The problem

The Philippines has a learning poverty rate of about 91 percent, and the gap starts with early literacy. At Future Minds Academy in Mandaluyong City, vocabulary and spelling lessons still depend on physical flashcards, teacher modeling and handwritten progress journals. Teachers carry a heavy preparation load and see progress data late.

What we built

An Android app and web portal for learners aged 5 to 7. Children scan objects around them and go through a scan, identify, pronounce, spell and feedback cycle with native-speaker Filipino and English audio. Teachers and parents get progress dashboards, messaging and consultation booking.

My role: Developer. I helped build both the web portal and the Android app, integrated YOLOv11n object detection (via Ultralytics) and speech coaching, and annotated the datasets used to train the detection model. The team worked in Agile Scrum and tested through alpha, beta, unit and system testing over three iterations.
