The problem

Flashcards don't scale

The Philippines has a learning poverty rate of about 91%, and the gap starts with early literacy. At Future Minds Academy, vocabulary lessons ran on paper flashcards and handwritten progress journals, so teachers saw problems late.

What we built

Learning from the real world

An Android app for learners and a web portal for the school. Kids learn words from objects around them, with native-speaker English and Filipino audio. Teachers and parents get dashboards, messaging and consultation booking.

My part

Vision, speech and both apps

I annotated the training datasets, integrated YOLOv11n detection through Ultralytics, and wired in speech coaching. I also built features on both the Android app and the web portal. We worked in Agile Scrum across three testing iterations.

The learning loop

  1. 01Scan

    Point the camera at an object.

  2. 02Identify

    YOLOv11n names it.

  3. 03Pronounce

    Hear it, then say it.

  4. 04Spell

    Type it on a kid-sized keyboard.

  5. 05Feedback

    Progress is saved for teachers.

02 · Computer Programming 1 final · Team project

WasteWatch

A console simulation of a smart camera that catches illegal dumping.

  • About 2,400 lines of object-oriented C++: classes for waste incidents, users and admins, each with its own manager.
  • When the "camera" detects dumping, it raises an alert with the waste type, location and time, color-coded by severity.
  • Personnel book an incident, then mark it resolved. Every step is logged with who handled it and when.
  • Separate admin and user logins, plus help, FAQ and privacy pages, all drawn in the terminal.
